# Why Math? — Product Brief

## Problem

Mass education historically asks many students to adapt to one lesson and evaluates how well they followed it. Learners have different background knowledge, vocabulary, interests, reasoning skills, and pace. Some students cannot formulate a question because they do not understand the explanation's language.

## Thesis

AI makes it possible to design and regenerate instructional experiences for an individual learner, with exceptional patience and low marginal effort. Teaching should adapt to the student—not the other way around.

## Experience

- Begin with a teacher-led, developmentally appropriate math concept sequence, rather than requiring a student prompt.
- Observe answers, questions, uncertainty, and demonstrated reasoning. Avoid inferring mastery from one answer.
- Generate explanations, analogies, visual demonstrations, interactive diagrams, and games as appropriate to observed understanding, not age alone.
- Keep mathematical claims and prerequisite relations grounded in a reviewed curriculum map and verifiable math logic.
- Build and update a personal Living Textbook automatically while the student learns. Separate confirmed facts from tentative understanding and misconceptions.
- Save learner state for repeat teaching, review, quizzes, and more advanced revisiting of prior concepts.
- Deliver Korean and English lessons. Demo narration and pitch should be in English.

## Product framing

**Headline:** Education has always asked students to adapt. It's time for education to adapt to students.

**One-liner:** An infinitely patient AI tutor that generates and adapts every lesson to each student's understanding, interests, and pace.

**Supporting line:** Same mathematical truth. A different path to understanding for every student.

## Longer-term vision

All ages, from foundational math learners and students to adults rebuilding conceptual understanding. Expand depth from arithmetic through algebra and calculus and eventually other domains. These extensions are not hackathon requirements.
