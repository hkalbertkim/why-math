# Why Math? — Hackathon judge Q&A (English)

The answers below describe the current prototype honestly. Do not use them as unsupported claims of educational effectiveness.

### Why not simply ask ChatGPT for a math explanation?

A learner may not know what to ask. Why Math begins the lesson, proposes a concrete learning objective, and updates its next teaching action from the learner's response. The individual Living Textbook records the interaction, so the experience is not only an isolated Q&A. The prototype has a small concept map; it is not yet a complete adaptive curriculum.

### What exactly is adaptive?

The **teacher engine is shared**. The AI chooses the next explanatory action from an observed response, such as defining terminology, using a concrete example, showing a number line, or checking a new application. The age of a test student does not select one of several hardcoded lesson systems.

### How do you measure whether a student understands?

The prototype records statements, questions, uncertainty and teacher judgments. It deliberately does not call one positive answer mastery. Its evidence and state estimates are still heuristic. Our next step is to validate the judgments with real learners and math educators.

### Does the demo show genuine student learning?

No. Students A and B are **AI simulations designed to test the software**, not human participants. Their hidden test profiles are not passed to the teacher. The demonstration shows that the system produces different next teaching actions; it does **not** prove better learning outcomes.

### Can the system teach every topic in mathematics today?

No. The hackathon prototype demonstrates a focused set of concepts around zero, the number line, and negative integers. The long-term architecture is intended to support broader mathematics, but broader coverage and accuracy are not yet validated.

### How do you stop an AI tutor from teaching incorrect mathematics?

The prototype includes a small reviewed set of mathematical facts and some targeted guards for obvious ordering errors and ambiguous understanding claims. These guards are not a general proof checker. Wider coverage will require deterministic validation, stronger evaluation and subject-matter review before deployment.

### Why have a Living Textbook?

The recorded material is an automatic byproduct of the lesson: explanations, examples, observations and open questions. It is meant to help a learner revisit concepts and let the tutor use prior context. It is not a certified mastery transcript.

### Can a learner use it in Korean?

Yes. Live sessions can switch between English and Korean; new AI responses use the saved session language while older messages retain the language in which they were originally written. This behavior was verified in a local browser test.

### Is the product ready for schools or minors?

No. This is a local hackathon prototype without production user management, abuse protection or school-specific privacy controls. We would not deploy it to real minors without additional safety, privacy and education evaluations.

### What would you validate next?

Start with a small number of real learners who struggle with specific concepts. Observe whether they can explain or apply the concept after the lesson. Compare the teacher-led approach against ordinary chatbot tutoring before extending the architecture or claiming educational impact.
