# Why Math? — Pitch and submission copy

## Five-second hook

Education has always asked students to adapt to the lesson. It is time for the lesson to adapt to the student.

## 20-second pitch (English)

Why Math? is an AI-led mathematics tutor that starts the lesson without requiring a learner to ask the perfect question. It observes how the learner responds, then changes its next explanation, example, or question. A Living Textbook records the learning conversation automatically.

## 60-second live intro (English)

Think about the last time you memorized a formula without understanding why it worked. Most classrooms have one explanation and many different minds. If a learner misses the meaning of the words, they may not even know what to ask. Why Math starts with the teacher, listens to the learner, and chooses a new explanation based on that conversation. To test the engine, we use two simulated fourteen-year-olds with different misconceptions. Their profiles are hidden from the teacher. What you see next is the actual app: two different student answers, two different teaching actions, and an automatically updated Living Textbook. This is an early prototype of the teaching loop, not proof of improved human learning outcomes.

## Event form: project name

Why Math?

## Event form: one-liner

An AI teacher that adapts the explanation to the learner — rather than forcing the learner to adapt to the lesson.

## Event form: short description

Why Math? is an AI-led, bilingual mathematics learning prototype. Learners can follow a guided concept path or begin through conversation. The AI teacher takes the initiative, observes responses and uncertainty, and adapts its next explanation and teaching action. An individual Living Textbook updates automatically. The hackathon demonstration uses two simulated students of the same age to test different gaps in understanding, a small implemented concept chain (zero, number lines and negative numbers), and a PNG-based interactive counting activity. It does not claim measured learning gains.

## Evidence, not claims

- Actual AI Teacher sessions can be shown live in English or Korean.
- The two simulated test learners use one shared teacher engine; their hidden test profiles are not passed to the teacher.
- Recorded examples are software test evidence, not a controlled educational study.
- Living Textbook records explanations and observations, not general mastery.
- Broad curriculum coverage and education efficacy are not yet established.
